Six habits that turn a career change into a career.

  1. 1

    Trust the process

    Follow the roadmap even when progress feels slow. Fundamentals compound.

  2. 2

    Work hard

    Build real projects, break them, and fix them. Hands-on time beats theory.

  3. 3

    Be consistent

    A little every day outperforms occasional marathons. Show up and ship.

  4. 4

    Stay hungry

    Cloud and tooling change constantly. Keep learning long after the first role.

  5. 5

    Network

    Share your work, join communities, and learn from people a step ahead of you.

  6. 6

    Help others

    Teaching what you know deepens it, and lifts the next person coming up behind you.
