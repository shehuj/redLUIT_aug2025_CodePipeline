# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do **not** open a public issue for security problems. Report them privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/shehuj/redLUIT_aug2025_CodePipeline/security/advisories/new)
and include steps to reproduce. You can expect an acknowledgement within 5 business days.

## Scope

- The static pages (`*.html`) and their third-party assets
- The CI workflow in `.github/workflows/`
- The AWS infrastructure in `infrastructure/` and `bucketPolicy.json`
